## Production Notes

### Sound Design Specifications

```yaml
audio_specifications:
  format: "Stereo, 48kHz, 24-bit"
  master_volume: "-16 LUFS (podcast standard)"

  narration:
    volume: "-20 dBFS peak"
    eq: "Slight warmth boost at 2-4kHz for clarity"
    compression: "Gentle, 3:1 ratio"

  music:
    volume: "-30 dBFS under narration, -20 dBFS solo"
    ducking: "Sidechain compression when narration speaks"

  sound_effects:
    volume: "-25 dBFS ambient, -15 dBFS dramatic"
    panning: "Use stereo field for immersion"

  character_voices:
    volume: "-18 dBFS"
    processing: "Slight reverb for magical characters"
```
