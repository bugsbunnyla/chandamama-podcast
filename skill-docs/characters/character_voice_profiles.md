# 🎭 Character Voice & Persona Profiles
## Country-Origin Character Mapping for 8 Languages

---

## Design Philosophy

> Characters from Sanskrit stories are **re-imagined** through the storytelling traditions of each target language's culture. They keep their core essence (bravery, wisdom, mischief) but wear the "voice costume" of their new cultural home.

### Transformation Rules
1. **Core traits preserved:** A brave prince remains brave; a clever fox remains clever
2. **Speech patterns localized:** How they speak matches the storytelling tradition
3. **Cultural references adapted:** Local equivalents for food, clothing, greetings
4. **Musicality matched:** Rhythm and cadence fit the language's oral tradition
5. **Visual design blended:** Traditional Indian elements + target culture's folk art style

## 🔊 Voice Casting Quick Reference

### Narrator Voice Requirements

```markdown
| Language | Gender | Age Range | Tone | Special Skills |
|----------|--------|-----------|------|----------------|
| English | Male/Female | 50+ | Warm baritone/alto | Can do Indian lilt |
| Telugu | Male | 40-60 | Theatrical baritone | Burra Katha style |
| Hindi | Male | 50+ | Deep, commanding | Kathavachan training |
| Chinese | Male/Female | 40-60 | Clear, resonant | Pingshu training |
| Spanish | Female | 50+ | Warm, melodic | Cuentera style |
| Italian | Female | 50+ | Expressive, musical | Commedia feel |
| German | Male | 50+ | Warm, precise | Märchen style |
| French | Male/Female | 40-60 | Elegant, whimsical | Conteur style |
```

### Character Voice Differentiation

```markdown
For each story, ensure:
- [ ] Each character has a distinct vocal range (pitch)
- [ ] Each character has a distinct speech rhythm
- [ ] Each character has a signature sound (laugh, sigh, catchphrase)
- [ ] Animal characters use appropriate sound effects
- [ ] Deity characters have slight reverb/echo
- [ ] Demon characters are deep but NOT nightmare-inducing
- [ ] Child characters are voiced by actual children or skilled adult mimics
```
