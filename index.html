About Chandamama

In 1947, a magical magazine called Chandamama ("Moon Uncle") was born in India. For decades, it told stories to children across the subcontinent — in Sanskrit, Telugu, Hindi, Tamil, Bengali, and more.

These stories carried ancient wisdom: lessons of dharma (doing what is right), ahimsa (kindness to all living things), and courage that comes in all sizes.

Now, from the December 1999 Sanskrit issue preserved on Archive.org, we are bringing these tales back — as free podcasts and interactive storybooks, in 8 languages, for children everywhere.
